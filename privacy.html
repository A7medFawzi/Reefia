Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us through reefiya.com.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Reefiya LLC (REEFIYA), an exporter of Egyptian sweet potatoes, 144 Port Said St, El Ibrahimaia Bahry, Alexandria, Egypt. For any privacy question, contact exports@reefiya.com.

What we collect

When you send a request for quotation (RFQ), we receive the details you enter:

  • Company name, website and country of registration
  • Contact person, job title, business email and phone number
  • Your product, packaging, volume and shipping requirements, and any notes you add

We do not ask for payment details or sensitive personal data. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we receive what you send there.

How we use it

We use your information only to review your enquiry, prepare a commercial reply and quotation, and communicate with your company about it. We send you a confirmation email with a PDF copy of your request. We do not sell your information or use it for third-party marketing.

Legal basis

We process your details because you asked us for a quotation (steps before a possible contract) and because we have a legitimate interest in answering business enquiries and running our export business.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run this website and deliver emails:

  • Vercel: website hosting and privacy-friendly visit statistics
  • Resend: delivery of RFQ emails to our export team and to you
  • Google Fonts: web fonts (your browser requests them from Google)
  • WhatsApp: only if you choose to contact us there

These providers may process data outside your country. They act on our instructions and protect data under their own security terms.

Cookies and statistics

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Visit statistics come from Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. Your browser stores your language choice and a draft RFQ reference number locally on your device; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep RFQ details for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any business relationship that follows, and as required by law for commercial records. You can ask us to delete them earlier.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email exports@reefiya.com and we will reply within a reasonable time. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Access to RFQ information is limited to our export team and the service providers above. Our form is protected against spam and abuse, and data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
